Team

This page lists members, collaborators, and alumni of the Duke University group. Chris also leads an applied research team at NVIDIA; the two groups operate independently.

Principal investigator

Christian Dallago

Senior Research Scientist and Applied Research Team Lead, Digital Biology, NVIDIA.
Visiting Assistant Professor in Biostatistics & Bioinformatics, and Cell Biology, Duke University.

Chris earned his PhD in Informatics (summa cum laude) from the Technical University of Munich in 2023. His doctoral research focused on self-supervised representation learning for protein and nucleotide sequences (SeqVec, ProtTrans, Nucleotide Transformer), open-source protein analysis tools (bio_embeddings, PredictProtein), and standardized benchmarks and data-splitting protocols for protein fitness prediction (FLIP).

At NVIDIA, he has worked on computational biology methods and open releases spanning GPU-accelerated homology search (MMseqs2-GPU), proteome-scale complex structure prediction with the AlphaFold Database, atomistic generative models for protein and biomolecular design (Proteina, La-Proteina, Proteina-Complexa, RFD4-Proteina), and agentic workflows for functional binder design (BindClaw).

Since 2025, he holds a dual appointment as Visiting Assistant Professor at Duke University and Applied Research Team Lead in Digital Biology at NVIDIA. At Duke, his group works on machine learning methods for biological sequence modeling, molecular design, and rigorous evaluation; at NVIDIA, he leads applied research in computational biology and molecular engineering.
